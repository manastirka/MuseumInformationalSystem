## 💡 Tips for Collection Management

### Best Practices:
1. **Regular Communication**: Multi-curator collections need coordination meetings
2. **Clear Responsibilities**: Define who manages which specimens
3. **Documentation**: Keep detailed records of all specimens
4. **Preservation**: Follow best practices for each collection type
5. **Research Integration**: Link specimens to publications and projects

### Collection-Specific Notes:

**Botany (3 curators)**:
- Divide by specialty: flora, medicinal, ethnobotanical
- Regular herbarium maintenance
- Coordinate field collections

**Paleozoology (6 curators)**:
- Largest team requires clear coordination
- Divide by vertebrates/invertebrates or geological period
- Regular collection reviews

**Entomology (2 curators)**:
- Scientist handles identification and research
- Conservator handles preservation and preparation
- Close collaboration essential
